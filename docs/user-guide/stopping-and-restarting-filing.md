# Stopping and restarting GST/HST or QST filing

When a registration is cancelled, you become a small supplier, or the CRA or Revenu Québec
suspends it, stop filing in Numo9 so no new returns are expected after that date.

## Stop filing

1. Go to **Settings › Tax**, open the registration's **Filing periods**.
2. In **Stop filing**, enter the **last day registered**, the reason and a note.
3. Select **Review the impact**. Numo9 shows:
   - your final return — the period that ends on that day;
   - the unfiled periods after it that will be removed;
   - documents dated after it that still use GST/HST or QST codes, with links to review them;
   - that new sales after it default to **Out of scope** (you can turn this off under
     **Settings › Tax › Default codes › Sales after filing stops**).
4. Type the registration number exactly to confirm, then select **Stop filing**.

You can't stop before the end of a period you've already filed. Filed periods never change.

## While filing is stopped

The timeline shows a **Not registered** band from the day after the stop. An invoice or bill
dated in that band that charges or claims the registration's tax shows a warning in the editor:
change its tax code, or check its date.

## Restart filing

Select **Restart filing**, enter the day you're registered again (after the last day registered)
and your filing frequency, review the preview, then select **Restart**. Periods start again on
that date; the days in between stay **Not registered**.

Every stop and restart is listed under **Registration history** with who made it and when.
