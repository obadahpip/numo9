# Empty, loading, error and explain states

A-10-T12. Every list and screen has all four states; none of them redirects silently.

## EmptyState

<Canvas of={States.Empty} />

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    <p>
      Say what will appear here and give 1–3 actions: "No contacts yet" → **Add contact**, **Import
      contacts**.
    </p>
    <p>A different message for "no results" with a way to clear the filters.</p>
  </Do>
  <Dont>
    <p>A blank grid, or "No data".</p>
    <p>An upsell in the empty state.</p>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## Skeleton

<Canvas of={States.Loading} />

Show a skeleton shaped like the content as soon as loading takes more than 300 ms — never a
spinner alone. The skeleton announces "Loading …" to screen readers once.

## ErrorState

<Canvas of={States.LoadError} />

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    <p>Say what failed, keep what still works, and offer **Try again** and **Report a problem**.</p>
  </Do>
  <Dont>
    <p>"Something went wrong." with no next step, or a stack trace.</p>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## ExplainPanel

<Canvas of={States.Gated} />

For a refused action or route: not in the package (gated, 402), no permission (403), locked
period, or not found (404). It names the rule, who can change it and offers a one-click request.

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    <p>
      "Payroll isn't in your package (Starter). It's included in Growth and Complete." [Compare
      packages] [Ask the owner to upgrade]
    </p>
  </Do>
  <Dont>
    <p>Redirecting to Home or a sales page without explanation (Xero's silent redirects).</p>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## Gated (feature keys)

<Canvas of={Gated.GatedFeature} />

U-01-T06. `<Gated feature="payroll.pay_runs.write" locked={…}>` renders its content when the
organisation's package includes the feature key and the gated ExplainPanel **in place** when it
doesn't; `useFeature(key)` gives the same decision to a single control. Decisions come from the
nearest `FeatureProvider` (the U-03 entitlement store); without one every feature is allowed.

<Canvas of={Gated.Allowed} />

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    <p>
      Wrap the gated part only (the pay-run editor, the "Approve" button), name the product and the
      packages that include it, and keep everything else on the screen working.
    </p>
  </Do>
  <Dont>
    <p>Hide a gated menu item or button, or render the children while the decision is loading.</p>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
