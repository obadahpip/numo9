# Layout: app shell and org switcher

## AppShell

Top navigation by job (Home · Sales · Purchases · Banking · Accounting · Tax · Payroll ·
Inventory · Reports · Contacts), the org switcher slot, practice navigation for accountants,
breadcrumbs, user menu, notification bell and job tray (A-10-T05). Responsive 360–1440 px: below
1024 px the navigation moves into a drawer.

<Canvas of={Shell.Organisation} />

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    <p>Put the page's title in an `h1` at the top of `main`, right under the breadcrumbs.</p>
    <p>
      Send system notices (maintenance, finished jobs, trial reminders) to the notification centre
      behind the bell; background jobs show progress in the job tray.
    </p>
  </Do>
  <Dont>
    <p>
      Promotional or announcement banners above editors or lists — they push the work below the fold
      (gap-12; blocked by the `numo9/no-work-screen-banners` lint rule). Commercial messages belong
      in Settings → Subscription or the Home announcement slot only.
    </p>
    <p>A second page banner — the shell's top bar is the only `banner` landmark.</p>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
